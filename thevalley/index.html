The reveal

One estate. One couple. One weekend.

Not one ballroom. Not one ceremony lawn. Not a collection of unrelated venues. Larger estates in this region run two and sometimes three weddings on a single Saturday. Seventy-four acres cannot be split, so they are not.

Every celebration includes every space — Magnolia House, The Valley, the Lookout Deck, Davis Hall, the trails, the meadows and the lodging on the hill.
