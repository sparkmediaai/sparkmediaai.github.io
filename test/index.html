01

Buy the intent

Search

We bid on call-now queries inside your exact service radius and the hours you can actually run. Emergency modifiers. Tight geos. No “best plumber in America” vanity terms.

  • Signals: burst searches, “near me”, after-hours
  • Constraint: dayparts match dispatch capacity

Pick up the phone

Answer

Calls, forms, and chat hit an always-on receptionist. Target pickup is seconds, nights included. The homeowner hears a human-speed greeting — not a hold queue.

  • Coverage: 24/7, 12-second answer target
  • Route: agent → dispatcher → tech

Protect the truck

Qualify

Service area, urgency, and job type are confirmed before anyone rolls. Junk leads die here. Real jobs keep their context — address, issue, window.

  • Checks: radius, urgency, job class
  • Output: a brief your dispatcher can use

Put it on the board

Book

The appointment lands on your calendar with SMS confirmation. Spend stays on the queries that book. You dispatch. We keep the loop closed.

  • Hand-off: calendar + context, not a voicemail
  • Loop: booked jobs feed the next day’s bids
