Welcome Two hours Request

From Paul & Kobi Cummings

Wildwood, Georgia

If you are looking for a place that becomes yours for a weekend —

The wedding weekend,
reimagined.

We do not begin with a list of rooms, or a number of acres, or a folder you can download. We begin with a simple offer: come to the estate. Spend two hours here with Kobi. See whether these days should belong to you.

From Friday until Sunday the gates are, in a manner of speaking, yours. Guests who sleep on the property — about thirty-two can — wake in the same air they celebrated in. That is the whole idea. The rest is hospitality.

We keep this place as a father and a daughter. We would rather know how you want the weekend to feel than tell you how a venue is sold.

With warmth,
Paul & Kobi

What you are actually requesting
