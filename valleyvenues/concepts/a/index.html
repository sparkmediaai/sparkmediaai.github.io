Why a weekend

A wedding may be one of the only times in your life when nearly everyone you love is gathered in one place.

Family. Childhood friends. Grandparents. People who live across the country. People who haven't seen each other in years. At most venues, all of that is compressed into six scheduled hours.

Everything feels easier

More of what you need is already here.

Fewer separate decisions

Tables, chairs, linens, and an in-house decor library are already on the property, so fewer details become their own vendor, their own invoice, and their own phone call.

Your day survives the weather

Indoor and outdoor spaces sit minutes apart. A forecast that changes on Thursday doesn't change what your wedding feels like.

You experience it instead of running it

Setup, cleanup, golf carts, parking, security, and coordination happen behind the scenes. Guests see the magic, not the machinery.

Everyone you love can stay

Onsite lodging for up to 34 guests keeps the people closest to you a short walk away, all weekend.

A world away, surprisingly close
