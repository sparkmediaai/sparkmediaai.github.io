Written against the Brand and Website Direction: one estate, one couple, one weekend, one wedding at a time, with Lookout Mountain leading and capacity and pricing held back until later. Two things in these pages are decisions rather than facts — leading with THE VALLEY instead of The Valley Venues, and the wording of Kobi’s credentials, which needs word-for-word approval under the NDA.

Photography is the estate’s own. A few spaces — the conservatory at Magnolia House, Overlook Village, the cottage in the woods — have no photography yet and are stood in for by other parts of the property.
