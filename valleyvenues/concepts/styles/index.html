Valley Venues · Design study

Five directions for the same page

Each is The Valley, with identical content — so the only thing that differs is the design. Look at whichever feels most like the estate, not whichever has the most going on.

01

Scroll-Responsive

The hero is a slot onto the meadow that opens as you scroll. Nothing plays at you; it responds to you.

Cormorant Garamond + Montserrat
02

Nightfall

The estate after the reception, lit by lamplight. A lantern follows your cursor across the page.

Libre Baskerville + Manrope
03

Field Guide

The land observed and annotated. Aspect, best light, wet plan — real facts in the margin.

Fraunces + Outfit
04

Stone & Glass

The built estate rather than the landscape. A survey datum line runs across every section.

DM Serif Display + DM Sans
05

The Drawn Estate

The map is the hero. You meet all seventy-four acres first, and The Valley as one point on them.

Marcellus + Jost
Photography throughout is the estate's own. Some spaces — the conservatory, Overlook Village, Lost in the Woods — have no photography yet and are represented by other parts of the property.
