The season guide

What you are actually booking, month by month.

The estate is open April through November. Each stretch has its own argument, and none of them is the wrong answer.

April – May

Everything is new

Wildflowers through the meadow, the hardwoods still opening. Cool enough that nobody is uncomfortable in a jacket.

June – August

Long, generous light

Sun on the grass until nearly nine. Ceremonies push later, cocktails run long, and the deck earns its keep.

September – October

The ridge turns

The most requested weeks on the calendar, and the reason. Gold by five, and cool by the time you sit down to dinner.

November

Bare and enormous

The leaves go and the ridgeline shows its shape. Fires lit, the hall warm, and the whole valley visible through the trees.

Whichever you choose

The aisle runs whichever way the light is better that month.

Nothing here is fixed — not where you face, not where your family sits, not what stands behind you in the photographs. And if the forecast turns on the Thursday, Davis Hall is four minutes away with the same guest list and none of the panic.
