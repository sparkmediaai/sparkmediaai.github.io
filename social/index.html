Why UGC

People trust people.

Polished brand videos get skipped. A tech on a job site, a homeowner showing the fix, a quick “here’s what we found” — that reads like a friend’s post. It earns the watch, the follow, and the call.

  • Native to the feedVertical, fast, captioned, sound-on or off. Built for how Instagram actually plays.
  • Shows the real workYour crew, your trucks, your jobs. Proof you can’t fake with stock footage.
  • Doubles as ad creativeThe same clips can run as paid ads on Instagram and Facebook — cut to length with hooks up front.
  • Keeps you top of mindWhen the emergency happens, they already know your name and face.
