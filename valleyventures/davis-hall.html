Davis Hall

The Venue

Made for the moment everyone remembers

Davis Hall is a versatile event venue that is perfect for any occasion. This grand reception hall boasts a spacious main area with room for up to 250 attendees, an attached food-staging area, and a state-of-the-art sound and lighting system.

Its bright, neutral palette and clean industrial lines make it a canvas for any vision — from candle-lit black-tie to garden-party whimsy — so your celebration flows effortlessly from first dance to last toast.
