Our Story

A family estate, generations in the making.

Tucked into the rolling countryside of Wildwood, Georgia, The Valley Venues is a family-owned retreat where 74 private acres unfold into meadows, woodlands, and mountain views — just ten miles from downtown Chattanooga.

What began as a beloved family homestead has become one of the region's most enchanting settings for weddings and gatherings. Every path, porch, and pasture is thoughtfully tended so that your day feels both grand and wonderfully personal.

Paul & Kobi Cummings Owners · A Father-Daughter Partnership
