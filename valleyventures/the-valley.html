The Valley

An open-air ceremony lawn where golden hour does the decorating.

The Venue

Vows beneath an open sky

This expansive green space — once a beloved horse-riding arena — has been thoughtfully transformed into a captivating event venue, framed by an array of vibrant flowers and surrounded by lush forest. As the sun sets, a warm, magical light envelops the lawn, creating a truly enchanting atmosphere.

From ceremony to cocktail hour to a reception beneath the stars, The Valley is as versatile as it is beautiful — an equally perfect setting for weddings and corporate gatherings alike.
