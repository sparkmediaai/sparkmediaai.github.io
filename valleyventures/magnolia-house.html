The Crown Jewel

History, romance & enchantment

What truly sets The Valley Venues apart is the Magnolia House — a hallmark building that seamlessly intertwines history, romance, and enchantment. Originally built in the 1890s, this beautifully restored home is our crown jewel and a breathtaking backdrop for any special occasion.

The house has been closed since the fire on May 7, 2025, and the rebuild is officially underway. Preserving its timeless elegance and charm while introducing thoughtful new touches, the transformation will be more breathtaking than ever — reopening in 2026.
